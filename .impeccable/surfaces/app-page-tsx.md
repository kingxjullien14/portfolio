# Surface brief: portfolio home (`/`)

## Scope and mode

Single-route portfolio home at `/`. Visitor mode: Experience. The work itself leads from the first viewport.

## Audience, job, action

- Audience: peers, founders and potential collaborators in or near clean energy, logistics and fintech, arriving from GitHub, LinkedIn or a shared link on laptop or phone.
- Job: decide within a minute whether Jullien ships serious software, then dig into one or two systems.
- Action: open a conversation by email (secondary: LinkedIn, GitHub, CV download).

## Proof and content

- Seven units: Vendor App (Flutter), Trading Portal, Vendor Portal, WWS Dashboards, API Platform (FatHopes work), plus OSAI and Stone and Chisel (Jullien's own products).
- Every FatHopes interface is a recreation with synthetic data, labeled as such. No real names, prices, volumes, logos or URLs from company systems.
- OSAI credit is binding: "Based on Firaz Fhansurie's AIOS". OSAI repo is public (MIT); Stone and Chisel is live at stonenchisel.com, repo private.

## Constraints

- Keep Lenis smooth scrolling and Revelo-style text motion (split-text reveals, scroll read-along); add more on top.
- Zero em or en dashes in visible copy. No eyebrow labels above headings. Amber only for lit state.
- Reduced motion collapses every effect to a settled, readable state; content is visible by default.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

Dispatch Room. The memorable moment is the split-flap board flipping Jullien's name in above seven live units, each row a way into its bay.

## Unresolved decisions

- Portrait treatment on the operator card (monochrome print by default).
- Night shift defaults to the system colour scheme; the toggle overrides it per visit.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is the dispatch room of the network Jullien builds: every shipped system is a unit in service on a split-flap board. It refuses the category default of a dark gradient hero, glass project cards and a skills grid.

OWN-WORLD: Stoved-enamel grey-green panels, graphite trim and ink, a black split-flap board with off-white glyphs, amber lamps as the only warm colour and only for lit state, engraved tags in expanded Archivo, condensed Archivo on the flaps, one flap-cell module ruling every gutter. Night shift turns the enamel deep graphite-green.

STORY: Visitors see all seven systems at once, grasp the kitchen-to-refinery flow they serve, inspect each as a live recreated interface with Jullien's own handover note, then open a channel by email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim graphite console strip with nav keys and a night-shift toggle. Below it a full-width board housing: JULLIEN NAZREEN flips in on large flaps, FULL-STACK DEVELOPER beneath, then seven unit rows (unit, platform, stack, status lamp). Under the board, the one-line thesis at left; "View the work" and the amber "Email me" button at right.

FORM: Dispatch Room, number 3 on my ordered list of seven, seed key 82769f81. Signature interaction: the split-flap board, reused as bay headers and a now-showing nav display. Motion grammar is mechanical (flap flips, lamp warm-ups, shutter reveals) over Lenis, with Revelo-style type.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
